Codex
The accompanying Codex-LICENSE.txt and Codex-NOTICE.txt are from the official
rust-v0.162.0-alpha.17.2 tag:
https://github.com/openai/codex/tree/rust-v0.162.0-alpha.17.2
Read and retain those exact license and notice documents. The four bundled
Windows executables retain their existing signed-container bytes, sizes and
SHA256 values in codex/manifest.json. Their raw hashes differ from the matching
official npm archive members. A reviewed PE comparison found program content

terminal signature table. This is not a claim of raw official-file equality.
No signature validation is performed by this text or by the inventory audit.

libusb
libusb-1.0.30-COPYING.txt supplies the upstream LGPL2.1 terms.
Both source archives are included beside this notice, not merely linked:
  libusb-1.0.30-source.tar.gz
  libusb-package-1.0.30.0-source.tar.gz
The second archive is the pyocd/libusb-package v1.0.30.0 build wrapper and recipe;
the first supplies libusb v1.0.30 source for its src/libusb submodule. To inspect
or rebuild independently, extract the wrapper and place the library source at
its src/libusb layout, retaining the upstream COPYING and notices. Follow the
wrapper README, setup.py, pyproject.toml and build-wheels workflow rather than
inventing a binary-equivalence claim. Matching upstream submodule/tag commit:
87a55632db62c9bdc58cd31d3ccfa673f1bb017f.
https://github.com/libusb/libusb/tree/v1.0.30
https://github.com/pyocd/libusb-package/tree/v1.0.30.0
